# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.
